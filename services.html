Our Services

One connected ecosystem for stronger agriculture.

BhuSanchar connects farmers with agricultural products, soil-testing support, local stock points, logistics, technology and agricultural knowledge.

01. Quality Agricultural Products

Quality inputs, closer to every farmer.

Explore composition, benefits, suitable crops, application, packaging, storage and applicable quality or registration information.

02. Farmer Help Card

One card. More benefits. More savings.

Registered farmers can access digital ID, QR verification, soil-testing support, local stock points, seasonal offers and purchase history, with eligible savings of up to 25%–50%.

Get Farmer Help Card
03. Find Stock Point

Agricultural support, closer to you.

Search by state, district, block, panchayat and village, or find a BhuSanchar point near you for products, services, directions and delivery availability.

Find a Stock Point
04. Our Network

From one block to every corner of India.

A scalable structure connecting States and Union Territories, districts, blocks, panchayats, villages, stock points and farmers.

Explore Our Network
05. Free Soil Testing

Know your soil. Make better decisions.

Farmer registration, soil-test request, sample collection, laboratory testing, soil report and farmer guidance, subject to program availability and applicable terms.

Request Soil Test
06. Farmer Support

Support that comes closer to the farmer.

Get help with products, usage, soil testing, stock-point availability, Help Card, schemes, quality concerns, orders, deliveries and general assistance.

07. Government Scheme Support

Understand available opportunities.

Explore scheme benefits, eligibility, documents, application processes and official government resources. Approval and disbursement remain with the relevant authority.

From source to block. From block to farmer.

Product availability, inventory visibility, stock planning, transportation, block-level fulfilment and farmer or customer delivery coordination.

Farmer profiles, digital cards, soil reports, support requests, inventory, billing, reports, stock alerts and field-officer task management.
