Our Team

People working closely with farmers and rural communities.

BhuSanchar is being built by a team that brings technology, business, agriculture, operations, distribution and rural-market capabilities together to create a farmer-first ecosystem.

Our People

A network built from the field up

District Level

District officers and coordinators manage regional operations, network development and performance.

Block Level

Block officers coordinate stock points, farmer engagement and local operations.

Panchayat Level

Panchayat and field officers work directly with farmers and support grassroots activities.

Agriculture & Soil

Agriculture professionals and soil-testing teams support farmers with practical field guidance.

Sales & Distribution

Teams manage product reach, local relationships and demand generation across rural networks.

Logistics & Operations

Operations teams ensure movement of products from source to stock point and farmer/customer.

Technology

Technology professionals develop the digital infrastructure connecting farmers, partners and the network.

Corporate Team

Business, finance, HR, marketing and administration teams support the overall organization.

Vijay Kumar

Founder — BhuSanchar

Vijay Kumar is building BhuSanchar with the vision of combining technology, agricultural distribution and grassroots farmer support into one scalable ecosystem.

The objective is to create a business that doesn't simply distribute agricultural products, but builds a long-term network around farmers, local entrepreneurs and agricultural communities.
